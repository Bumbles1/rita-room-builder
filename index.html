❓ How to play

👆 Tap a thing in the list to put it in the room.

🖐️ Drag it to move it. Drop it on top of other things to stack them!

🔄 Turn, resize and recolour with the buttons and sliders that pop up when you pick something.

🧱 Build your own bunk bed: add a Tall frame, drop a Mattress on top, add a Ladder and a Safety rail. Put a Desk or Cushion nook underneath.

📐 Fold-down desk: it lives on the wall. Use the switch to fold it up or down.

🧗 Play stuff: climbing walls and cargo nets stick to walls, monkey bars, swings and ropes hang from the ceiling. Drag them along the wall or ceiling.

🪟 Share the room: use a curtain, shelf or half wall to split it. Turn on the halfway line in 🎨 Room to see each half.

🎥 Look around: drag on empty space to spin the room, pinch or scroll to zoom. Try the Top and Door views.

⌨️ Keyboard: R turn, arrows nudge, Delete remove, Ctrl+Z undo.
