# Rita's Room Builder – backlog

Ideas and requests that are not built yet, roughly in order.

## Modes (Sleep / Play / Homework)
One room that switches between set-ups instead of separate saved designs.
- A row of mode buttons in the top bar, e.g. 🌙 Sleep, 🎮 Play, 📚 Homework, with an "add a mode" button.
- Each mode remembers where every item is and which fold-away things are open or closed
  (fold-down desk up, trampoline tucked away, curtain divider drawn).
- Switching modes slides and spins the furniture into place with a short animation, like a tiny-house transformation.
- New items added in one mode appear in every mode; deleting removes it everywhere.
- Saved designs keep all their modes.

## Smaller ideas
- Real mirror on the "Mirror" decor item (same planar reflection as the wardrobe doors).

- Measure tool: tap two points on the floor to see the distance.
- Share a design as a link or QR code so it can be opened on another device.
